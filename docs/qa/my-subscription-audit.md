# My Subscription bug audit and fixes

Date: 8 October 2026. Base: `ba5143a3d1d5e97d2182ff7ba535f36862690a2f` (master). Live tenant and authorized platform administrator screens were inspected read-only. Every mutation below was reproduced/verified against synthetic tenants in a separate PostgreSQL/Moqui stack. The live system, credentials, invoices and tenant subscriptions were not changed.

The user's chosen policy is **immediate downgrade**, with no mid-cycle refund; existing paid-period dates are retained. This overrides the next-renewal downgrade assumption in SOP-08. Customer recurring Subscriptions is outside this task.

## Verification and limits

See [runtime README](my-subscription-runtime/README.md) and its evidence for commands, assertions, browser results and cleanup. Four repository source guards, JavaScript syntax and diff whitespace checks are required. The standard master staging acceptance workflow is dispatched with the exact candidate SHA; CI results belong to that candidate, not a mocked page.

The Razorpay order and captured-payment lookups follow the official [order fetch API](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/orders/fetch-with-id/) and [payment fetch API](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/payments/fetch-with-id/). Signed local fixtures do not prove a real gateway charge. Keep the PR draft until test-mode checkout and its callback/webhook have passed, and require owner review/no-op deployment gate. No claim that all possible bugs or production errors have been eliminated.
