## Remaining defects and release gates — do not merge/deploy as “all bugs cleared”

1. **GSTN filing schema incomplete:** B2B invoice-type codes, May-2025 HSN B2B/B2C split/UQC, CDNU, document/nil sections and turnover must be reconciled against the current official schema and tested using the official offline utility. JSON is explicitly a draft. Unregistered notes stop draft generation instead of silently omitting liabilities. No upload/filing was attempted.
2. **Provider integrations unavailable:** real e-Invoice/e-Way/Avalara/TaxJar adapters, credentials, sandbox acceptance and cancellation/amendment reconciliation are not implemented. Removing fake success prevents false compliance records; it does not implement connectivity. E-Way threshold checks remain indicative and need state/supply/exempt-goods policy review.
3. **Tax policy incomplete:** generic exemptions are not automatically applied by the India calculator; product `isGstExempt` is the supported path. Partial exemption/RCM/SEZ policy needs an approved definition. Missing slab fallback and HSN validity require owner decisions and master-data reconciliation before altering live billing.
4. **GSTR-3B is a reconciliation draft:** RCM liability, eligible ITC/reversals, Tables 3.2/5 and electronic-ledger offsets are not fully implemented. Recorded input tax must not be called eligible ITC or final payable.
5. **Historical data reconciliation:** legacy POS-linked invoices without item GST fields can fail reconciliation. Do not backfill issued documents automatically. Validate representative legacy documents and all raw reporting consumers read-only before rollout.
6. **TDS/TCS:** current-law section/rate/threshold matrices and actual ledger postings need statutory review; legacy simulated auto-deduction remains an unimplemented flow. Current 206C(1H) is disabled, not replaced by another levy. Legacy deduct/deposit changes were excluded because their invalid section/rate fields need a complete withholding regression pass.
7. **Coverage gaps:** stock-transfer caller, full GSTR2B upload/duplicate/date cases, simultaneous writes, very large tenant performance, non-India tax/provider flows, Safari/Edge/a11y keyboard pass and tenant records with legacy/shared ownership need targeted checks. This is not evidence that every module has no bugs.
8. **Production no-op gate:** synthetic POS, Sales and Purchase checks do not prove zero changes to future MARUTHI tax outcomes. Senior review must approve changed inclusive rounding, GSTIN checksum rejection, issued-status selection and fail-closed provider behavior. No deployment is authorized by this report.
